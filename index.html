The pain point

Regulators ask for reasons. Compliance teams write them by hand, in English.

RBI, IRDAI, SEBI and the IndiaAI Mission now expect AI explainability for credit, underwriting and healthcare decisions. No tool writes regulator-ready explanations in Hindi and regional languages, so every model decision gets a manual, English-only justification.

Why now

Best practice is becoming a mandate

IndiaAI's governance guidelines and RBI's FREE-AI framework are turning explainability into a compliance requirement, with audit deadlines already approaching.

The window

A narrow first-mover gap

No multilingual XAI product exists in India. Global players have not localized yet, and that will not last.

Proposed solution

Audit-ready explanations, generated automatically, in 22 Indian languages.

It plugs into an existing credit, underwriting or diagnostic model through an API, extracts feature-level reasoning, and turns it into a compliance narrative in the format the regulator expects.
